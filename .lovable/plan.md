- Header block: name, office, Column #1, campaign site, contact email, deadline (Aug 28, 2026).

Everything is drafted from what is already public on the site, so nothing new is committed on his behalf:

- Intro / why running: his own first-person account in `CANDIDATE_STORY` — East Brunswick upbringing in a working-class immigrant family, EBPS K-12, close to six years in a special education placement he worked his way out of, summa cum laude in psychology, master's in data science, data work as a career.

- Being heard: the site's own "ask a question, get an answer" model, student representatives to the board policy, and pre-debate question intake.
