# Coherence repair pass

## Goal
Fix sentences that became awkward, vague, or logically disconnected during the plain-language rewrite. Preserve the candidate's meaning and every sourced fact.

## Confirmed problems
- Replace malformed phrases such as “Universally paid public full-day Pre-K” with “Universal, publicly funded full-day Pre-K.”
- Replace unclear metaphors such as “Grade students on their work, not their homes” with a direct statement about limiting homework’s effect on grades.
- Clarify vague lines such as “Replace buildings when repeated repairs cost more” by naming the comparison: repair cost versus replacement cost.
- Rewrite “Use state construction grants to build for lower maintenance” so the funding source and the goal are separate and understandable.
- Fix compressed review-room fragments such as “what a bridge program adds on top of existing bilingual staffing” and incomplete “Still unknown” entries.
- Remove headings or transitions that no longer match the text beneath them.

## Method
- Read each sentence with the sentence before and after it; edit the whole passage when a one-line substitution would break the logic.
- Keep the public priorities brief, but use complete statements that a voter can understand without background knowledge.
- Keep formal policy and budget terms in the review room, followed by a direct explanation when needed.
- Do not introduce new arguments, promises, figures, or political claims.
- Do not force every sentence into the same short cadence. Use enough connective language to preserve cause and effect.

## Verification
- Review every changed passage in its rendered section, not as an isolated string.
- Check public and review pages for fragments, unclear pronouns, unsupported transitions, and contradictions between summaries and details.
- Confirm the site builds cleanly and that mobile layouts still fit.
