# Take the site offline (temporarily)

Goal: the campaign site stops serving to the public, with everything kept intact so it can go back up later.

## What I can't do from here

Unpublishing is not something I can trigger. It has to be done by you in **Project settings → Publishing → Unpublish**. That makes the published Lovable URL and both custom domains (saqeeb.org, saqeebforeb.com) stop serving.

## Steps

1. You click Unpublish in Project settings. The published URL and custom domains go dark.
2. Custom domains stay connected in settings (DNS unchanged), so re-publishing later brings them straight back — no re-setup.
3. Alternative if you're on a Business/Enterprise plan and want the site reachable to your workspace only instead of fully dark: I can flip publish visibility to private, which keeps the URL alive but requires workspace login. Say the word and I'll do that instead.

## What stays intact

- All code, pages, and content remain in the project.
- The database keeps everything: voters, households, turfs, canvass responses, questions, volunteer signups, review notes.
- Admin, canvassing, and review tools remain in the codebase; they simply aren't reachable while unpublished.
- The preview URL still works for you and anyone with project access.

## To bring it back

Publish again. Nothing else needed — same content, same domains.

## Optional add-on

If you'd rather visitors see a short notice ("Campaign site is temporarily offline") instead of a dead domain, tell me and I'll add a holding page and republish with only that page live. Otherwise we go fully dark.
